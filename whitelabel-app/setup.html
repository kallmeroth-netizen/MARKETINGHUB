Step 5 · Connect your data

Add your API keys

Paste an API key to connect a source, or leave it blank — anything empty simply shows as "Not connected" and you can wire it up later from Admin.

Powers the calendar's Add to Google Calendar button — the ICS/feed URL your team subscribes to.
No API? Use a Google Sheet

Keep your numbers in a Google Sheet and the hub reads them — no developer needed. In your sheet: File → Share → Publish to web → (choose the tab) → CSV, then paste that link here and hit Test. Update the sheet by hand or automate it (Apps Script, Zapier, Make) — the hub always shows the latest.
